`cyz-edu-research` adapts selected non-commercial workflow ideas from the locally installed Academic Research Suite, especially:

- reproducible search and two-pass screening;
- the `three-way-scan` frame (`WHY / HOW / WHAT` plus cross-paper synthesis);
- annotated bibliography, literature matrix, cross-paper tension, and source-verification concepts.

## qu-ai-wei (Historical Adaptation)

The academic humanization module adapts selected concepts from the locally installed `qu-ai-wei` Skill, including genre-aware diagnosis, density-based pattern detection, fact-preservation priority, overcorrection protection, Chinese syntax and rhythm repair, and auditable polishing reports.

Original copyright: Copyright (c) 2026 @LifelongLazyLearner.

Original license: MIT License. The complete license text is included as `third_party/qu-ai-wei-LICENSE.txt`.

This package substantially narrows and modifies the original workflow for Simplified Chinese academic manuscripts. It adds section-specific rewrite intensity, frozen academic anchors, deterministic semantic-lock comparison, post-rewrite integrity gates, and explicit protection for uncertainty, causal boundaries, negative evidence, methods, statistics, citations, and research ethics. General-audience requirements that could conflict with academic prose, such as forcing personal “rough edges,” are not applied unchanged.
